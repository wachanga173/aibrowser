Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Verifiable Zero Telemetry Guarantee

Privacy Guard is designed under a non-negotiable architectural commitment: zero remote data transmission. The extension, background scripts, and local AI engines do not contain analytics, remote logging, telemetry trackers, or background telemetry pings.

2. Local Data Processing

All page content analysis, ad/tracker filtering, and AI summarization occur entirely on your local machine. No raw web page HTML, extracted text snippets, or user prompt queries leave your browser.

3. Local Storage & Encryption at Rest

Any data generated during browsing sessions (such as local activity logs or preference vectors) is stored strictly in your browser's local storage or encrypted local data directories. All local vector data is encrypted at rest using keys derived from your operating system's secure keychain.

4. Human Gate Action Isolation

The extension enforces strict code-level permission boundaries. Sensitive actions (such as form submissions or navigating outside approved scopes) cannot be triggered automatically by AI models and require an explicit single-use user click confirmation token.
