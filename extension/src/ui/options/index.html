Terms & Privacy Policy Framework

1. Verifiable Zero-Telemetry Data Sovereignty

Privacy Guard operates under a strict, non-negotiable architectural framework: Zero outbound data transmission. All DOM parsing, ad/tracker filtering, local vector indexing, and AI prompt processing execute 100% locally on your computer. Zero metrics, URLs, or prompts leave your device.

2. Local Storage & Encryption at Rest

All logs, blocking metrics, and local vector embeddings are stored strictly in local storage (chrome.storage.local) or local data directories. Vectors are encrypted at rest using keys derived from your operating system's native keychain.

3. Direct Action Validation

All browser actions execute within strict schema allowlists ensuring safe, sandboxed interaction with web elements.
